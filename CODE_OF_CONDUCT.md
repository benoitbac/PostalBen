# Code of Conduct

## The short version

PostalBen is a game about a man losing his patience with other people. The project is not.

## Our standards

Behaviour that's expected:

- Assume good faith. Most disagreements are about context, not intent.
- Critique the work, not the person. "This breaks the pacifist path" is useful; "this is
  stupid" is not.
- Accept that maintainer decisions on tone and scope are final, and that "no" is a complete
  answer.

Behaviour that isn't tolerated:

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks, public or private
- Discriminatory language or imagery — including in contributed game content
- Sexual attention or advances of any kind
- Publishing others' private information without permission
- Deliberately derailing discussions or wasting maintainers' time

## About in-game content

This project makes satire, and satire has targets. The line is documented in
[`docs/GDD.md` §5](docs/GDD.md): the game punches at institutions, bureaucracy, and people
who make other people's lives harder on purpose. It does not punch at ethnicity, religion,
sexuality, gender, or disability.

Arguing that the line should move is a design conversation and it happens in issues.
Ignoring the line and submitting content across it is a conduct matter and it gets closed.

"It's just a joke" is not a defence. The whole project is jokes; that's why the aim matters.

## Scope

Applies in the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions, and anywhere someone is
representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report to **bacotben@gmail.com**. Reports are handled privately and in confidence.

Responses, escalating with severity or repetition:

1. A private word explaining what was wrong
2. A warning, with a period of no interaction with the people involved
3. A temporary ban from project spaces
4. A permanent ban
